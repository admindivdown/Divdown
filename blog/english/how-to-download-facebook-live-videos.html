How to Download Facebook Live Videos After Broadcasting

August 2026 • 1 min read

Live broadcast replays on social platforms are great to catch up on, but keeping a local copy of a long stream can be tricky once the broadcast ends.

You do not have to rely solely on cloud replays when you want permanent offline access to past live events.

Easy Replay Steps

Step 1
Copy the share URL of the completed live video stream.

Step 2
Launch https://divdown.net through any web browser.

Step 3
Paste your link into the input box and trigger the Download process.

Your media file will compile and save securely.
