How to Download Facebook Stories to Your Phone

August 2026 • 1 min read

Stories on social platforms vanish after twenty-four hours, leaving you no native way to keep memorable moments shared by friends.

If you catch a great clip running on someone's daily feed and wish to store it permanently, you will need a reliable workaround.

Saving Stories in 3 Quick Steps

Step 1
Copy the specific link pointing to the story stream you want to preserve.

Step 2
Navigate to https://divdown.net using your mobile browser.

Step 3
Insert the copied address into the designated field and tap Download.

Your media file renders instantly for offline viewing.
